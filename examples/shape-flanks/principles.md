# shape-flanks: principles

**Look.** White, a condensed serif headline with one italic word, geometric confetti dissolving in from both edges, a mono audience toggle and a mono black pill.

## Why it works
- **Atmosphere:** Shape flanks: squares, circles, quarter circles, triangles and diamonds on a 22px grid, dense at the edges and gone by 24% in.
- **Headline emphasis (exactly one move):** One italic word in the serif headline.
- **Signature (repeated 2–3 times):** The shape grid, repeated as the logo mark.
- **Color strategy:** Full palette at the edges (coral, aqua, sky, sun, black); the center stays black on white.
- **One primary action** in the first viewport, with at most one quiet secondary.
- **The preview proves the headline** instead of decorating it: None above the fold; the logo wall and the shape field carry the first screen.

## Rules it keeps
- Real nouns and numbers in the copy, no hype words, no exclamation marks.
- 4px spacing grid, `gap` instead of margin stacks, the hero rhythm and viewport budget from SKILL.md section 9.
- Headline at 2–3 lines on desktop, `text-wrap: balance`, a `max-width` in `ch`.
- Craft floor: skip link to `main#main`, `:focus-visible` ring, `theme-color`, labels and `aria-hidden` on decoration, `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- At 390px: no horizontal scroll, no collisions, full-width CTAs where they stack.

## Against the undecided defaults
This example follows every undecided default, so it can be copied as is when the user hasn't stated preferences.
