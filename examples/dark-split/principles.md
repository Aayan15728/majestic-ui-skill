# dark-split: principles

**Look.** Dark left/right split, a beige highlight block, a laurel badge, a hand-drawn blob listing what's inside, a big yellow button and a scarcity line.

## Why it works
- **Atmosphere:** Dark split: copy left, a hand-drawn outline right holding the contents.
- **Headline emphasis (exactly one move):** Highlight block on the second phrase.
- **Signature (repeated 2–3 times):** Hand-drawn blob outline plus the highlight block.
- **Color strategy:** Committed: yellow accent on #1f1f1f.
- **One primary action** in the first viewport, with at most one quiet secondary.
- **The preview proves the headline** instead of decorating it: A hand-drawn blob of feature tiles.

## Rules it keeps
- Real nouns and numbers in the copy, no hype words, no exclamation marks.
- 4px spacing grid, `gap` instead of margin stacks, the hero rhythm and viewport budget from SKILL.md section 9.
- Headline at 2–3 lines on desktop, `text-wrap: balance`, a `max-width` in `ch`.
- Craft floor: skip link to `main#main`, `:focus-visible` ring, `theme-color`, labels and `aria-hidden` on decoration, `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- At 390px: no horizontal scroll, no collisions, full-width CTAs where they stack.
