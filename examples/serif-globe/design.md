# serif-globe: design

**Brief.** Get creators and software makers to move their store over by showing a checkout that already handles tax. The order summary proves it.

| | |
|---|---|
| Brand (invented) | Tillpoint, checkout, tax and payouts for digital products |
| Page title | Tillpoint — sell anything digital, keep more of every sale |
| Visitor mode | Persuade |
| Use it for | payments, commerce infrastructure, developer platforms with a premium feel |
| Not for | bright playful brands |

## First viewport (1440 × 900)
Grid nav with a serif wordmark, a NEW chip, a two-line serif headline at 96px, a three-line lead with the fee, a white pill and an outline pill.

See `web.jpg` for the desktop render and `mobile.jpg` for 390 × 844.

## Page structure
1. Hero over the globe.
2. Serif sub-heading and a row of eight assistant tiles (invented marks).
3. Checkout frame: product with a stippled object drawing, order summary with VAT and total, pay button.

## Components
- **CTA:** White pill + outline pill, 56px.
- **Proof:** Fee line in the lead; tax countries under the pay button.
- **Preview:** A full checkout with order summary.

## Mobile (390px)
- Links hide; only the primary pill stays in the nav.
- Globe shrinks and recenters.
- Tiles wrap to 4 × 2.
- Checkout stacks product over summary.

## Copying it
Copy `index.html`, keep the structure and spacing, and replace the brand, copy, data and (if the user has their own) tokens and fonts. All names, numbers and quotes in the file are placeholders.
