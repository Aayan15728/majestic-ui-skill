# riso-hills: principles

**Look.** A risograph print: grainy mustard paper, halftone cream clouds, hatched green hills and a tiny walker, with a heavy caps headline and cream and black pills.

## Why it works
- **Atmosphere:** Risograph scene: SVG turbulence grain, halftone dot patterns in the clouds, hatched and displaced hill layers.
- **Headline emphasis (exactly one move):** A size and case jump: sentence-case line, then a heavy all-caps line at 1.6×.
- **Signature (repeated 2–3 times):** Halftone dots and hatching, repeated in the logo's waveform line.
- **Color strategy:** Drenched: the mustard paper is the color, with cream, blue and greens from the print.
- **One primary action** in the first viewport, with at most one quiet secondary.
- **The preview proves the headline** instead of decorating it: The print is the moment; follow with a before/after player.

## Rules it keeps
- Real nouns and numbers in the copy, no hype words, no exclamation marks.
- 4px spacing grid, `gap` instead of margin stacks, the hero rhythm and viewport budget from SKILL.md section 9.
- Headline at 2–3 lines on desktop, `text-wrap: balance`, a `max-width` in `ch`.
- Craft floor: skip link to `main#main`, `:focus-visible` ring, `theme-color`, labels and `aria-hidden` on decoration, `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- At 390px: no horizontal scroll, no collisions, full-width CTAs where they stack.
