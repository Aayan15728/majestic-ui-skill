# clear-sky: design

**Brief.** Get people to download the desktop app. The preview shows the product working over a real call, so the promise is visible in one look.

| | |
|---|---|
| Brand (invented) | Brieflark, a meeting assistant that writes notes and suggests answers live |
| Page title | Brieflark — meeting notes and answers while you talk |
| Visitor mode | Persuade |
| Use it for | desktop and mobile consumer apps, assistants, productivity tools that run beside other apps |
| Not for | dense B2B dashboards, or brands that need a flat, quiet first screen |

## First viewport (1440 × 900)
Nav with three links and Log in on the sky; a two-line Geist Serif headline at 80px; the fading hairline; a 19px white lead; one glossy button and fine print; the desktop preview rising over the peaks.

See `web.jpg` for the desktop render and `mobile.jpg` for 390 × 844.

## Page structure
1. Hero on the painted sky.
2. Desktop preview: wallpaper, toolbar pill, call window, suggested-answer card with notes.

## Components
- **CTA:** One glossy blue button with an OS icon, 48px tall, radius 12, plus a line of fine print naming the other platform and the free tier.
- **Proof:** The answer card quotes a real-looking question and an answer with two highlighted facts.
- **Preview:** A desktop scene: warm wallpaper with rays, a dark pill toolbar, a four-person call and the assistant's glass card.

## Mobile (390px)
- Nav links hide; Log in stays.
- Headline 44px in three lines; the button goes full width.
- The sun drops lower and smaller; peaks show between the fine print and the preview.
- The call window keeps its title and recording state; notes hide under the answer card.

## Copying it
Copy `index.html`, keep the structure and spacing, and replace the brand, copy, data and (if the user has their own) tokens and fonts. All names, numbers and quotes in the file are placeholders.
