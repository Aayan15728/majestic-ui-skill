# stream-lines: principles

**Look.** Warm white, soft lanes of glyph traffic converging on one hairline card, model tiles at the lane mouths, blue routes with square nodes, a two-tone headline with a blue second line.

## Why it works
- **Atmosphere:** Model streams: periwinkle and aqua lanes carrying monospace glyphs, curving in from both edges to the card.
- **Headline emphasis (exactly one move):** Two-tone: the second line in the accent blue.
- **Signature (repeated 2–3 times):** Square nodes on hairlines, on the routes and on the card edges.
- **Color strategy:** Restrained: ink on warm white, one solid blue used for the second headline line, the button and the routes.
- **One primary action** in the first viewport, with at most one quiet secondary.
- **The preview proves the headline** instead of decorating it: The card itself is the interface; the streams are the system.

## Rules it keeps
- Real nouns and numbers in the copy, no hype words, no exclamation marks.
- 4px spacing grid, `gap` instead of margin stacks, the hero rhythm and viewport budget from SKILL.md section 9.
- Headline at 2–3 lines on desktop, `text-wrap: balance`, a `max-width` in `ch`.
- Craft floor: skip link to `main#main`, `:focus-visible` ring, `theme-color`, labels and `aria-hidden` on decoration, `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- At 390px: no horizontal scroll, no collisions, full-width CTAs where they stack.
