# riso-hills: design

**Brief.** Get video editors to upload a cut and hear a fitted soundtrack. The scene sets the mood; the 40-second promise is the hook.

| | |
|---|---|
| Brand (invented) | Scorecraft, AI music written to fit a video |
| Page title | Scorecraft — music written to fit your video |
| Visitor mode | Experience |
| Use it for | creative tools, music, film, media and anything with a warm, made-by-hand voice |
| Not for | enterprise and finance |
